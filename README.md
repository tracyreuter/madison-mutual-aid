# madison-mutual-aid

## Contribute

These are just a few examples of networks and initiatives in Madison, Wisconsin:

### [Madison Mutual Aid Network](https://madisonman.coop/)
The Madison Mutual Aid Network (MAN) is a new type of networked cooperative, designed to create means for everyone to discover and succeed in work they want to do, with the support of their community. (Join us on [Discord](https://discord.gg/qYaMM6qgn).)

### [Sustainable Hill Farms](https://www.sustainablehillfarms.org/)
Sustainable Hill Farms is a community-driven initiative focused on promoting biodiversity, sustainability, and collaboration in our neighborhood and beyond.

### [Dane County Neighbors Helping Neighbors - Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/groups/daneconeighbors/about)
Welcome to your community group! Meet your neighbors, keep up to date on the latest community news. Find answers to your questions and enjoy a safe space to provide and seek assistance if needed!

### [Madison Food Not Bombs - Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/fnbmadison)
This group may be inactive. It could be restarted, or start a new one with the [FNB start-up plan](https://foodnotbombs.net/new_site/seven-steps.php).

### [Madison Public Library - Seed Library](https://www.madisonpubliclibrary.org/collection/special-collections/madison-public-library-seed-library)
Madison Public Library's Seed Library includes two FREE seed giveaways throughout the year... The seed library helps create a culture of sharing and community, and saving seeds leads to a sustainable future for both your community and your garden.

### [Little Free Library](https://littlefreelibrary.org/)
Our mission is to be a catalyst for building community, inspiring readers, and expanding book access for all through a global network of volunteer-led Little Free Library book-exchange boxes.

## Read

Here are a few books

* *Mutual Aid: Building Solidarity During this Crisis and the Next* by Dean Spade
This book introduces mutual aid with a deserved sense of urgency.

* *Food Not Lawns: How to Turn Your Yard Into a Garden and Your Neighborhood Into a Community* by Heather Jo Flores
This book encourages people to replace lawns with organic gardens to grow nutritious food and strong communities.
