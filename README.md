## Contribute

These are just a few examples of networks and initiatives in Madison, Wisconsin:

### [Madison Mutual Aid Network](https://madisonman.coop/)
MAN is a new type of networked cooperative, designed to create means for everyone to discover and succeed in work they want to do, with the support of their community.

### [Sustainable Hill Farms](https://www.sustainablehillfarms.org/)
SHF is a community-driven initiative focused on promoting biodiversity, sustainability, and collaboration in our neighborhood and beyond. (Join us on [Discord](https://discord.gg/qYaMM6qgn) to keep in touch.)

### [Dane County Neighbors Helping Neighbors - Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/groups/daneconeighbors/about)
Meet your neighbors, keep up to date on the latest community news. Find answers to your questions and enjoy a safe space to provide and seek assistance if needed!

### [Madison Food Not Bombs - Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/fnbmadison)
This group could be restarted, or we could start a new one with the [FNB start-up plan](https://foodnotbombs.net/new_site/seven-steps.php).

### [Madison Public Library - Seed Library](https://www.madisonpubliclibrary.org/collection/special-collections/madison-public-library-seed-library)
Madison Public Library's Seed Library helps create a culture of sharing and community, and saving seeds leads to a sustainable future for both your community and your garden.

### [Little Free Library](https://littlefreelibrary.org/)
The LFL mission is to be a catalyst for building community, inspiring readers, and expanding book access for all.

### [Madison Freecycle](https://www.freecycle.org/town/MadisonWI)
The Freecycle Network is a grassroots movement of people who are giving (and getting) stuff for free in their own towns and keeping good stuff out of landfills.
